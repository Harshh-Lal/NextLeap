## **6. Page-Level Requirements** 

### **6.1 Home** 

- Hero section communicating what NextLeap IT Solutions does and who it serves 

- Summary/grid of all 9 services, each linking to more detail on the Services page 

- Trust-building elements (e.g., process overview, why-choose-us section) 

- Prominent call-to-action leading to Contact Us throughout the page (not just at the bottom) 

- Preview/teaser of latest Content section posts 

### **6.2 Services** 

- One dedicated section per service listed in Section 4 

- Each service section should be self-contained enough to be linked to directly (e.g., for ads or shared links) 

- Each service section ends with a call-to-action toward Contact Us or the relevant Package 

### **6.3 Packages** 

- Present package tiers by scope/inclusions and target use-case, not by price 

- Each package should map clearly to one or more services from Section 4 

- Call-to-action per package should route to Contact Us for a quote 

### **6.4 Content** 

- A listing page of articles/posts, filterable or categorized by service area if feasible 

- Individual content/article page template 

- Basic content management capability so new posts can be added without a developer 

### **6.5 Contact Us** 

- Lead capture form: name, business name, email, phone, service(s) of interest, message 

- Form submissions should be delivered by email and/or stored for follow-up (exact method to be decided with developer) 

- Direct contact details (email/phone) displayed alongside the form 

- Confirmation message or page shown after successful submission 

## **7. Design Requirements** 

- Visual identity should be built around the provided NextLeap IT Solutions logo (teal-to-blue gradient) shown at the top of this document 

- Clean, modern, professional look suited to a B2B service business 

- Fully responsive across desktop, tablet, and mobile 

- Consistent use of the logo in the site header across all pages 

## **8. Technical Requirements** 

- Fast page load times and good Core Web Vitals, given SEO is itself one of the services offered 

- On-page SEO fundamentals: meta titles/descriptions, semantic headings, sitemap.xml, structured data where relevant 

- Analytics integration (e.g., Google Analytics or equivalent) to track the success metrics in Section 2.1 

- Content section should be manageable through a CMS or admin interface, not hardcoded 

- Contact form should include basic spam protection (e.g., CAPTCHA or honeypot) 

- Hosting, domain, and stack to be proposed by the developer based on the above requirements 

## **9. Assumptions** 

- Final written copy, service descriptions, and package details will be supplied separately and are not part of this document 

- No e-commerce or online payment functionality is required for version 1 

- No client login/portal is required for version 1 — this is a marketing/lead-generation site only
